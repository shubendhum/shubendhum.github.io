### **Fostering Innovation at the ANZ UNConference**

As part of ANZ’s drive towards continuous innovation, the UNConference provided a platform for collaborative discussions and ideation.

### **Key Themes:**

- **Zero Trust Implementation:** Focused on strategies to embed Zero Trust principles into organizational security frameworks, aligning with industry best practices.
- **AI-Driven Solutions:** Discussed leveraging AI for real-time fraud detection and adaptive authentication mechanisms.
- **Customer-Centric Design:** Collaborated on ideas to enhance user experiences by integrating seamless and secure identity solutions.

### **Outcomes:**

The event led to actionable initiatives, including the development of advanced risk profiling techniques and AI-enhanced fraud prevention workflows. It also reinforced ANZ’s commitment to innovation and operational excellence in the digital identity space.
